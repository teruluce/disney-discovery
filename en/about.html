About Disney Discovery

Disney Discovery is an independent, fan-run site for people who want to learn a little more about Tokyo Disney Resort and Disney.

What you'll find here

We publish beginner-friendly park guides, practical planning tips, and approachable articles about park design, entertainment, characters and films. Our goal is to answer common questions clearly and help readers notice more on their next visit.

Editorial approach

We aim to distinguish confirmed information from interpretation and to keep articles clear and useful. Ticket rules, app features, schedules and park operations may change, so readers should check the official Tokyo Disney Resort website for the latest operational details.

Independent fan site

This website is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by The Walt Disney Company or Oriental Land Co., Ltd. Disney names, trademarks and character properties belong to their respective owners.
