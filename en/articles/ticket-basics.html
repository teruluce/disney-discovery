Tokyo Disney Resort Tickets: A Beginner’s Guide

Before buying a ticket, decide which park you want to visit, check the date and ticket type, and review the conditions shown at checkout. Prices and availability can vary, so this guide covers the basics rather than live ticket information.

The key point: Tokyo Disneyland and Tokyo DisneySea are separate parks. A standard park ticket is for the park and date stated on the ticket; do not assume it allows entry to both parks.

1. Choose your park and date first

Compare the two parks, then choose a date that works for your group. Check the official calendar and ticket sales page before making travel plans around a particular day. Opening hours, ticket availability and operating arrangements may change.

2. Understand age categories

Ticket prices are generally divided into age categories such as adult, junior and child. The applicable category depends on the visitor’s age under the resort’s stated rules. Check the current official ticket page for the exact age boundaries and any special conditions before purchasing, especially when travelling with children.

3. Buy through an official channel

Use the official Tokyo Disney Resort website or another sales channel explicitly identified by the resort. Review the park, date, ticket type, number of tickets and total price before confirming payment. Keep the ticket accessible in the official app or in the format provided after purchase.

4. Check the conditions before paying

  • Is the selected date correct?
  • Does each ticket match the intended park and visitor category?
  • What are the current change, cancellation and refund conditions?
  • How will each member of your group access their ticket at the entrance?

5. Prepare for entry

Install and prepare the official app if you plan to use digital tickets and park services. Make sure your phone is charged and that every member of your group knows how to access their ticket. Follow the instructions displayed by the resort for your visit.

Common mistakes to avoid

Do not rely on old screenshots for prices or age categories, assume that a ticket includes every paid service, or buy from an unfamiliar reseller without checking its legitimacy and conditions. Ticket admission and optional in-park services are different things.

Before your visit

Ticket rules and availability can change. Confirm the latest details on the official ticket page close to your purchase and visit date.
