PLAN YOUR VISIT

Tokyo Disney Resort for First-Time Visitors

A little preparation can make your first visit feel much easier. Use this guide as a starting point, then check official information for the date you plan to visit.

1. Choose the park that suits your day

Tokyo Disneyland and Tokyo DisneySea are separate parks with different themes, attractions and entertainment. If you have time for only one, compare the experiences that interest your group most rather than trying to do everything.

Read our comparison of Tokyo Disneyland and Tokyo DisneySea →

2. Check tickets and opening information

Review ticket availability, prices, operating hours and park notices before travelling. These details can change by date, so avoid relying on an old screenshot or a guide written for a different visit.

Tokyo Disney Resort official English website ↗

3. Prepare the official app

Check the current official app instructions before your visit and make sure your phone is charged. App functions, booking procedures and access rules may change; follow the instructions shown for your visit date.

4. Plan transportation and essentials

Allow time to travel to the Maihama area, reach the correct park entrance and pass through entry procedures. Bring comfortable shoes, weather-appropriate clothing and personal essentials. A portable phone battery may also be useful during a long day.

5. Leave room for surprises

Queues, weather and show schedules can affect your day. Choose a few priorities and leave flexibility for snacks, scenery, character encounters or an unexpected favourite.

A final reminder

This is a planning overview, not a live operations update. Confirm ticket rules, app features, attraction availability and entertainment schedules through official sources close to your visit.
