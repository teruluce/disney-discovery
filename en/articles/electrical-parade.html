Tokyo Disneyland's Electrical Parade Dreamlights

When evening arrives, the park's nighttime atmosphere changes. Electrical Parade Dreamlights is a long-running signature of Tokyo Disneyland, known for its illuminated floats and familiar Disney stories presented after dark.

What is Electrical Parade Dreamlights?

It is a nighttime parade featuring illuminated floats and Disney characters. Light, music and movement combine to create a different experience from daytime entertainment. The exact line-up, music, schedule and operating status can vary, so check the current official information for your visit date.

Why watch after dark?

The glowing floats stand out against the evening sky, while the surrounding park becomes part of the atmosphere. If you have already spent the day exploring attractions, the parade can be a chance to pause and enjoy the park from a different perspective.

Plan your viewing calmly

  • Check the day's entertainment schedule and any weather-related announcements.
  • Arrive with enough time to find a permitted viewing area, following cast member instructions.
  • Be considerate of other guests and keep pathways clear.
  • Bring clothing suitable for the evening weather and be prepared for the temperature to feel different after sunset.

Seasonal versions and changes

Entertainment offerings can change over time, including seasonal presentations or special versions. Do not assume a particular version will run on every date; check the current schedule and official announcements.

Make it part of your day

Rather than planning the whole day around one show, consider how the parade fits alongside your favourite attractions, meals and the time you want to spend exploring. A little flexibility makes it easier to enjoy the night.
